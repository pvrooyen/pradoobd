## 4. Things still to verify (the secondary inspection list)

### Priority A — engine internals (under-bonnet checks, 5 minutes)

1. **Oil filler cap** — open, look at the underside. Cream/mayonnaise residue = head gasket → walk away.
2. **Dipstick** — pull, wipe, reinsert, pull again. Photograph. Should be amber-to-black, not milky. Smell it: diesel smell = injector leak. Oil level *above* the max mark = fuel dilution = injector trouble.
3. **Coolant header tank** (engine cold only) — open. Oil film floating = head gasket. Sniff: diesel exhaust smell = head gasket. Colour should be pink/red/orange depending on coolant type, clean.
4. **Rear of cylinder head** (right side as you look at engine, near bulkhead) — look for wet/oily area. That's the **vacuum pump**, a known 1KD-FTV weak point that leaks oil. Repair ~5–10k MZN.
5. **VIN plate** on the firewall (back wall of engine bay) — photograph and verify it matches the livrete chassis number **KDJ120-0072377**.

### Priority B — low range test (procedure)

Auto box with mechanical secondary lever:

1. Stop completely.
2. Foot on brake (safety interlock).
3. Auto box D → N. *Not P* (P locks the transfer case).
4. Push the small transfer-case lever firmly from H to L. May feel stiff.
5. Dash indicator "L4" / "4LO" should come on **solid** (not flashing).
6. If it won't engage: hold the lever against L, take foot off brake briefly so car rolls 10–20 cm — it'll usually clunk in.
7. Auto box back to D, drive off. Engine will rev high at very low road speed — that's correct.
8. Test by crawling over a small berm or kerb at idle. Walk over with no throttle = low range + centre diff working.

If the lever absolutely refuses, it's a seized linkage (free up + grease, ~1–2k MZN) or a dead vacuum actuator on the transfer case — but on a *lever* model, it's almost always linkage.

### Priority C — service history docs

- **Timing belt receipts at 150,000 km and 300,000 km** — the 1KD-FTV uses a belt with 150k interval. Belt snap = bent valves = bricked engine. If no receipts, **assume due and budget the 25k MZN**.
- **Injector replacement receipts** — set may have been replaced at ~150k km. If no record, budget ~80k MZN risk.
- **General service book.**

### Priority D — cold-start + road test

- **Cold start** (engine stone cold): listen for excessive injector chatter, knock, or blue smoke. Glow plugs slow to fire = swap for 6–8k MZN.
- **Drive under load**: turbo response should be smooth, no whistle/whining, no flat spots, no blue smoke under acceleration (= turbo seals).
- **Steering / suspension** on rough surface: clunks from bushings, sag, wandering.
- **Aircon** — actually, Pierre is **skipping aircon** in the repair budget. Don't budget for it but worth noting if it doesn't blow cold.
- **Centre diff lock button** (on the dash) — engages locks centre diff. Test it briefly on dirt; should bind on tar (normal).

### Priority E — bodywork sanity

- **Bonnet gaps** — stand at the front, check gap between bonnet and each wing is symmetric. The right-side respray hides what may be older repair work; bonnet gap is the tell.
- **Look at all four tyres' DOT date codes** — replace anything older than 2020 regardless of tread.

## 6. 1KD-FTV — engine reference

### Known weak points (this car, Euro III, 250k km)

1. **Injectors** — common-rail piezo; service interval ~100–150k km. Replacement ~10–15k MZN × 4. Likely due if not done. Symptoms: rough idle, smoke, hard cold start, oil rising on dipstick (fuel dilution).
2. **Vacuum pump (rear of head)** — leaks oil. Cheap fix (~5–10k MZN).
3. **Timing belt** — 150k km interval. Snap = bent valves = engine ruined.
4. **EGR cooler crack** — coolant into intake. White smoke + losing coolant with no visible leak.
5. **Turbo VNT carbon** — power loss / hesitation.
6. **Glow plugs** — slow cold start.

### What this 2005 escapes (vs later years)
- ❌ **NO cracked-piston defect** — that's the 2007+ Euro IV with redesigned piston. This 2005 has the Euro III piston with metal-fibre reinforcement.
- ❌ **NO DPF** — added 2010+. No regeneration drama.
- ❌ **NO AdBlue / SCR.**
