# Prado — 1-month post-repair visual check

**Target date:** ~2026-06-15 (one month after the big repair round, ~3 weeks after Ponta Malongane).

**Why this checklist exists:** freshly-done repairs have a settling-in window where leaks or issues re-emerge. Catching them in the first month is cheap; catching them later isn't.

---

## At-risk checklist — ordered most-likely-to-fail first

### 1. Fresh leak spots under the car (overnight check)
Park on clean concrete or cardboard. Next morning, look for fresh spots and where they sit relative to the engine bay:

- **Front centre / behind the bumper** → turbo oil feed line. If the mechanic ended up using silicone instead of new crush washers, this is where it shows.
- **Driver-side near firewall** → power steering hose / pump.
- **Middle of engine** → vacuum pump (rear of head), oil pan, or front main seal.
- **Under the radiator** → coolant. New water pump area.

Any fresh wet patch → photograph it + the drip point on the engine.

### 2. Engine oil — dipstick (engine cold ≥2 hours)
- Level **between MIN and MAX**. Above MAX = injector leaking diesel into the oil (1KD-FTV signature).
- Colour: amber-to-black is fine. Milky / cream → head gasket.
- Smell: strong diesel smell on the oil → injector leak.

### 3. Coolant header tank (engine **cold**, never hot)
- Level at **FULL** mark. Below = leak from new water pump, hose, or weep elsewhere.
- Oil film floating on top → head gasket.
- Colour same as right after the flush.

### 4. Power steering reservoir
- Check level. New seals can need a small top-up after a few weeks of running.

### 5. Tyres (cold pressures + wear pattern on the 2 new ATs)
- Inner-edge wearing fast = alignment off, redo.
- Edge scuffing = alignment off.
- Check DOT date code on the 2 retained tyres — anything older than 2020 should be replaced.

### 6. Front CV joint (left side, the one replaced)
- Drive slowly in tight circles, both directions.
- Clacking gone at full **right** lock = repair successful ✅
- New clacking at full **left** lock → the right-side CV is now the next one due.

### 7. New noises at idle + on a 10-min drive
- Belt squeal at start = aux belt tension off (easy fix).
- Whining from the new water pump = early failure → mechanic warranty.
- Diesel knock louder than before = injector wear progressing.

### 8. Aircon (was deferred)
- Still blowing cold or weaker than a month ago? Gas leaks reveal themselves over weeks.

### 9. Suspension on a familiar bumpy road
- New clunks over bumps → deferred bushings worth doing now.

### 10. Bodywork — right-side respray
- Any paint lifting, bubbling, or colour shift? Substandard respray fails fast in coastal humidity.

### 11. Interior plastics
- Any new cracks developing around the radio, dash, cubbyhole?

## When you do this check

Walk this list with the notes in `docs/vehicle/` (this file + `briefing.md` + `notes.md`).

---

## Decision rules (so you don't have to think on the day)

- ✅ **Clean across all 11** → schedule the next check 2–3 months out, settling-in window has passed.
- 🟡 **Minor weep (oil/PS, very small)** → photograph, monitor for another month.
- 🔴 **Active drip / level dropping fast / oil milky / coolant has oil** → back to the mechanic immediately, don't drive far.
