## Why this car for Inhambane

A Prado 120 D-4D is the gold-standard family 4x4 for this part of Mozambique:
- **Full-time 4WD** with centre diff-lock, **low-range** transfer case (~2.57:1).
- Factory **wading depth 700 mm**, ground clearance ~220 mm.
- Approach / departure ~32° / 25°.
- 7-seater, big cargo, roof rails — handles family + gear.
- Comfortable on the long tar runs to Maputo / Beira.
- Diesel range ~700 km on a tank.

For sand, bush tracks, and the occasional river crossing it'll out-perform almost anything newer or smaller.

## What makes 600k MZN suspicious

Real Mozambican Prado sellers know what they have. A 40% discount in this segment doesn't happen by accident. Likely explanations, in order:

1. **Drivetrain tired** — bottom-end knock, slipping auto, dead injectors, leaking head gasket. Could be 100–200k MZN of catch-up work.
2. **Accident-rebuilt** — chassis straightening, hidden airbag light, mismatched panels.
3. **Documents trouble** — livrete not in seller's name, IPVA in arrears, unpaid import duty, stolen-recovered marker.
4. **Distress sale** — divorce, relocating, urgent cash. Real but rare at this discount.
5. **Just rough cosmetically** — heavy rust, smashed interior. Usually only 20% off, not 45%.

## Engine notes — 1KD-FTV (good news)

The famous **cracked-piston defect is a Euro IV problem (2007–2012)**. This 2005 has the earlier **Euro III piston with the metal-fibre reinforcement** — that's the better design.

Doesn't mean it's bulletproof at 250k km. Watch for:
- Injector wear — service interval ~100k km. Ask for receipts.
- Timing belt — replace every 150k km. When was last done?
- Vacuum pump on engine block (known weak point — leaks oil)
- Turbo seals (blue smoke under load = turbo)
- EGR carbon build-up (can cause rough idle / power loss)

## 2026-06-16 — Front suspension sitting too low (both sides)
Front of car significantly low on **both** sides after the front coil springs were removed and refitted. So low the wheels contact the front mud flaps over minor sand bumps. No new parts to be bought.
- Confirmed: 120-series Prado is **coil sprung front AND rear — no torsion bars** (verified via web). No ride-height adjustment on a coil; height is set by the spring.
- A drop this large is too big for a missing rubber insulator (~10–20mm). Prime suspects, in order:
  1. **Wrong springs in front — front/rear coils swapped** during the job (Prado front vs rear coils differ). → Compare the two front springs against the two rear (free length, wire gauge, coil count).
  2. **Spacer/packer left out** on top of each front coil (lose 30mm+ per side, evenly).
  3. Both coils badly unseated from their perches.
- Key unanswered question: were the **rear coils also removed** in the same job? If yes → front/rear swap is prime suspect.
- ⚠️ Don't keep driving like this: tyre-to-body contact + wrong front CV joint angles can damage CVs.

## 2026-06-16 — Exhaust smoke observation
Symptoms (1KD-FTV 3.0 D-4D): grey smoke ~20–30s on cold start (Moz warm climate, so not just condensation); slight grey haze under acceleration when warm.
- **Key context: injectors were RECENTLY REPLACED.** This reframes the diagnosis — see below.
- Most likely cause given recent injector work: **injector compensation/ID codes not programmed into the ECU** (1KD Denso common-rail requires coding via Techstream after injector replacement), OR poor-quality/reman injectors, OR an install fault (leaking injector seat/copper washer).
- Check order: (1) confirm injectors were coded in with Techstream; (2) injector brand/quality (genuine Denso vs aftermarket); (3) injector seat washers/leak; (4) only then air filter / intake (EGR) carbon as pre-existing causes.
- Watch-for escalation: haze turning black (fuelling), blue + oil consumption (oil/turbo), white & sweet w/ coolant loss (head — 1KD known issue).
