## Issue history

- **May 26** — Left for Ponta Malongane. Both front wheels hitting the mudflaps immediately (from the coil refitting). After ~400 km the engine light came on and the engine shut off. Mechanic diagnosed injector.
- **May 27** — Car returned running; mechanic said he replaced the injector again and warned the engine light might return (it never has since). Immediately found: all electric windows no longer working properly, and aircon dead (fans fine).
- **May 31** — On the way back from Ponta, a radiator pipe was severed due to incorrect placement on the earlier job — lost all coolant.
- **Jun 01** — Pipe repaired, refilled with plain bottled water (not coolant). On the drive back the yellow oil-pressure light came on — odd given the coolant issue. Light has persisted since: comes on after a few km, never on cold start.
- **Jun 07** — Windscreen replaced; aircon-not-switching investigated at the same time. Aircon fixed, but then the fans stopped working.
